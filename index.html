Privacy Policy for Gallery

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Gallery (package name in.shubhamchopde.simplegallery) is a photo and video gallery app for Android, developed by Shubham Chopde. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short: Gallery does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information the app accesses

To show your photos and videos, Gallery asks for Android's photo and video permission (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and older). With it, the app reads the photos and videos on your device and their basic details (file name, date, size, dimensions, folder).

This information is used only inside the app on your device, to display, sort, search and play your media. You can allow access to all photos and videos or only selected ones, and you can withdraw permission at any time in Android Settings.

2. Information the app stores

Gallery saves a few things locally on your device only:

  • Your settings (for example grid size and hidden folders).
  • Your Favorites list.
  • Where you stopped in a video, so playback can resume.

This data never leaves your device, is excluded from Android cloud backup, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. No internet access, no data collection

Gallery does not request the internet permission and cannot connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting services and no third-party tracking. We do not collect any personal or usage data, and nothing is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

4. Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. When you choose to share a photo or video, open it in another app, edit it in another app, or set it as a wallpaper or contact photo, Gallery hands that file to the app you pick through Android's standard system screens. What that other app does with it is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

5. Deleting photos and videos

When you delete a photo or video, Android asks you to confirm. On Android 11 and newer, deleted items go to the system trash ("Recently deleted"), where you can restore them or delete them permanently.

6. Children

Gallery does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information at all.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
